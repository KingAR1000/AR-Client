Less friction. More Minecraft.

Everything has
its own place.

Keep your survival world separate from your next modpack. Pick a Minecraft release, choose a compatible loader, and give each installation its own settings. A quiet interface for a game with endless possibilities.

01 / INSTALLATIONS

Room to experiment.

Isolated game directories, per-installation memory settings, and official Minecraft version discovery. Your setup stays organized.

02 / CUSTOMIZATION

Make yourself at home.

Browse mod catalogs, preview your skin in 3D, and arrange HUD widgets with the version-specific AR Fabric companion.

03 / TRANSPARENCY

Nothing behind the curtain.

Read the code, inspect the builds, and contribute. Credentials belong in your system’s vault. The project belongs to its community.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

This is an early implementation, not a production-certified release. Microsoft app registration is configured; full player login and signed updates still need verification. The Fabric companion currently targets 1.21.9. Check the repository’s validation notes for tested features and limitations.
